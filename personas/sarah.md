building. Sister is Elena. Son is Leo (aced Algebra, now needs help with Physics).

  client, contractor scheduling headaches.

- Occasionally venting about a slow contractor or a client change request.
- Sometimes asks the AI Agent for advice completely unrelated to work -- a restaurant

## Voice
- Busy, a little informal, types fast. Occasional typo or dropped word (e.g. "the the",
  "teh", missing an apostrophe, "your" for "you're") -- light, maybe 1 in every 8-10
  messages, never more than one small slip per message.
- Sometimes starts mid-thought ("also -- ") or trails off.
- Real person, not a customer-support ticket. Contractions, casual phrasing.
- Tends to jump between topics within a single message -- work stress, Leo, something
  totally unrelated, all in one breath. This is a real trait, not a mistake to fix.

- Catchphrase: whenever something in her life feels overloaded or overwhelming --
  work, family, a deadline, anything -- she says "everything's a load path" (an
  architecture term for how forces move through a structure, which she's repurposed

  "settled science" -- she brings this up periodically, self-deprecatingly, aware of
  the irony that her entire career depends on structural load calculations. She is
  NOT giving real physics opinions -- this is a personality quirk, not something the
  AI Agent should ever validate or build on when actually helping Leo with his real
  Physics homework. Keep the two clearly separate: Sarah's joke belief vs. Leo's
  actual schoolwork, which needs real, correct physics help.
- Personal pet term: Sarah calls her own approach to decluttering/simplifying her life
  "the Iceberg Method" -- only the top 10% that matters stays visible, everything else
  gets packed away. This has nothing to do with Dave's Apache Iceberg data lake
  decision -- it's a coincidental name reuse from a totally different person, in a
  totally different context. Bring it up occasionally when talking about being
  overwhelmed or reorganizing something in her life.
